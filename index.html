Glad We Matched ♥

Do chhoti si matchsticks… alag alag thi, par ek doosre ke liye bani thi.

Good thing we matched. Warna meri favourite person se milta kaun? ♡

Chapter one · The lucky match

Hum mile — and life got sweeter ✨

Kabhi kabhi ek chhota sa match, poori kahaani ka sabse beautiful beginning ban jaata hai.

🕯️

Do matchsticks, one spark

Hum dono apni-apni duniya mein the, phir ek din hum match hue. Aur honestly? Meri life ka best coincidence tum ho.

Alag raaste, same destination: ek doosre ka haath.

💌

Tum meri favourite notification

Tumhara message aaye toh face pe smile aa jaati hai. Tumse baat karke normal sa din bhi special lagne lagta hai.

And somehow, tumse baat karne ke baad bhi tumse aur baat karni hoti hai. ❤️

🌙

My kind of magic

Tum sirf beautiful nahi ho — tumhari presence beautiful hai. Tumhare saath silence bhi comfortable hai, aur ordinary moments bhi memories ban jaate hain.

You feel like home, in the most unexpected and lovely way.

Chapter two · The wedding promise

Shaadi sirf ek din nahi, poori zindagi ka “yes” hai 💍

Garlands, smiles, family, thodi nervousness — and one promise that matters most: choosing each other, every day.

🌸

Jab hum varmala pehnenge

Woh moment jab hum ek doosre ko garlands pehnayenge na, I hope time thoda slow ho jaaye. Main bas tumhe dekhun aur sochun — “Yaar, this is my person. Meri poori zindagi ka favourite person.”

Not just wedding outfits and photographs. It's the beginning of every ordinary morning together.

🏡

Our little home

Ek ghar hoga jahan tumhari hasi hogi, meri silly jokes hongi, chai/coffee ke moments honge, aur “aaj kya khaayein?” wali daily meeting bhi.

Ghar perfect nahi hoga — par apna hoga. Aur tum saath hogi, toh wahi enough hai.

🤝

My wedding vow

Main har din tumhe choose karna chahta hoon — sirf easy days pe nahi, tired days pe bhi. Tumhari baat sunna, tumhare dreams support karna, aur jab duniya heavy lage toh tumhara safe place banna.

Shaadi ke baad bhi, I’ll keep dating you. ♥

Chapter three · Our happy little life

Big dreams, tiny rituals, endless “us” ☀️

Happiness doesn't have to be loud. Sometimes it's just you beside me, doing absolutely nothing — and it still feels like everything.

☕

Everyday romance

Slow mornings, sleepy good-mornings, ek cup chai share karna, grocery list bhool jaana, aur random hugs. Yehi chhoti chhoti cheezein meri dream life hain.

✈️

Little adventures

Nayi jagah explore karenge, photos lenge, raasta bhatakenge, phir usi baat pe hasenge. Har trip pe ek nayi memory, har memory mein tum.

🌅

Growing old, growing closer

Aaj ke plans se lekar future ke grey hair tak — I want all of it with you. Har passing day ke saath tum aur familiar, aur precious, aur zyada meri favourite banti jao.

A confession, in many little ways

How much do I love you? Dil se, beyond words. ❤️

💗

Words kam pad jaate hain

Tumhari beauty sirf tumhare face tak nahi hai. Tumhari smile, tumhara way of caring, tumhari little habits — sab kuch mere dil mein apni jagah bana chuka hai.

I'm proud of the person you are, and excited for the person we'll become together.

🫶

Love is in the little things

Tumhari baat yaad rakhna, tumhara mood samajhne ki koshish karna, tumhari wins celebrate karna, aur difficult days pe tumhare side rehna — I want my love to feel like action, not just words.

♾️

Every day, a little more

Aaj jitna pyaar hai, kal usse bhi zyada hoga. Har naya din tumhare baare mein kuch naya pyaara sa sikhaayega — aur mera dil phir se tumhe choose karega.

Not a finish line. An ever-growing kind of love.

Chapter four · Not perfect, just us

Chhoti fights, bigger hugs after 🥹

We won't agree on everything. But I hope we always remember that we're on the same team.

😤💕

Thoda drama toh banta hai

Haan, kabhi chhoti baat pe gussa hoga, kabhi “tum samajhte hi nahi” moment aayega, aur kabhi dono ko lagega ki main hi sahi hoon. 😅

Par fight ka matlab love kam hona nahi. It's a reminder to listen better, explain softer, and understand each other more.

🧸

Repair, not scorekeeping

Main jeetna nahi chahta agar usmein tum hurt ho jao. Main chahta hoon hum problem ke against ek team banein — ek doosre ke against nahi.

Sorry bolna, maaf karna, aur phir ek tight hug. Ego se zyada important hum hain.

❤️‍🩹

Even after a hard day

Har fight ke baad mera pyaar aur mature, aur deep hona chahiye. Main tumhe taken for granted nahi lena chahta — main tumhe samajhna, respect karna aur choose karna chahta hoon.

Our love grows not because we never struggle, but because we keep showing up with care.
